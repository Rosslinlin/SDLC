Description:

Summary

Provide the customer with a clear confirmation of a successfully created Open Term Deposit, the key details, and the next-step actions.

User Story

As an Open Term Deposit customer, I want to see a confirmation page after I confirm my reviewed term deposit, so that I understand the result and what to do next.

Business Context

The confirmation page presents the outcome and the key information the customer needs to continue.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-001: Given the customer has confirmed the reviewed term deposit, when the request succeeds, then the confirmation page displays the outcome and key details.
  • AC-002: Given the confirmation page is displayed, when the customer reviews it, then the available next-step actions are visible.
